## Offence Details

**Nature of Offence:** Homicide / Culpable Homicide (IPC Section 302)
**Date of Incident:** 13 October 2024
**Time of Incident:** Approx. 22:30 hrs – 23:15 hrs
**Location:** Flat No. 4B, Sunrise Apartments, Lane 7, Koregaon Park, Pune – 411001

**Brief Narrative:**
On the night of 13 October 2024 at approximately 22:30 hrs, neighbours heard
a loud altercation followed by silence from Flat 4B, Sunrise Apartments. The
body of the deceased, identified as Mr. Anil Sharma (Male, 38 years), was
discovered by the building security guard Suresh Patil at 23:10 hrs lying on
the kitchen floor. The deceased bore multiple stab wounds to the chest and
abdomen. A kitchen knife with blood stains was recovered near the body.
The main door of the flat was found ajar. CCTV cameras in the building lobby
captured footage of an unidentified individual leaving at 22:52 hrs. A
partial shoeprint in blood was found near the kitchen doorway. Latent
fingerprints were lifted from the kitchen counter and the door frame. A
mobile phone belonging to the deceased was found smashed on the floor.
Preliminary forensic examination was conducted at the scene by the
Government Forensic Science Laboratory (GFSL) team. All evidence items
were packaged and sealed at the scene.
